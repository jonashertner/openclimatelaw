================================================================================
LIKELY DATA ISSUES (worth validating)
================================================================================

[1] FILING DATES ARE YEAR-ONLY, STORED AS JANUARY 1  —  4,492 / 4,812 records (93%).
    filing_date is recorded at year granularity and defaulted to YYYY-01-01.
    Anyone who sorts or filters by filing date sees thousands of spurious
    "1 January" filings, and true within-year ordering is lost.
    Suggested check: does the platform expose date precision (a year-only flag),
    rather than a hard 01-01 date?

[2] APPARENT DUPLICATE / MIRROR RECORDS (same case, multiple family records).
    - Exact-title duplicates: "American Fuel & Petrochemical Manufacturers v.
      O'Keeffe" appears as BOTH Sabin.family.2153.0 AND Sabin.family.8546.0.
      (Verify whether these are true duplicates or distinct proceedings.)
    - Translation mirrors: the same case exists as separate English and
      translated records — e.g. "Juliana v. United States" (US) and "Juliana Youth
      v. United States of America (Jóvenes ex Juliana v. Estados Unidos de
      América)" (coded XA). They compete in search and split citation/influence.
    Suggested fix: a stable case-identity / dedup key (e.g. CPR import_id) to
    collapse duplicates and link language variants.

[3] INTERNATIONAL / REGIONAL CASES UNDER A CATCH-ALL JURISDICTION CODE "XA"  —  221 records.
    ICJ, ITLOS, ECtHR, IACtHR, etc. all share the non-granular jurisdiction_code
    "XA". (court_id distinguishes the body, but the jurisdiction code does not.)
    Both KlimaSeniorinnen v. Switzerland and the 2025 ICJ Advisory Opinion are "XA".
    Effect: you cannot filter international bodies by jurisdiction code alone.
    Suggested fix: body-specific codes (ICJ / ITLOS / ECTHR / IACTHR / …).

[4] POST-JUDGMENT / EXECUTION ITEMS TYPED AS "Decision" EVENTS.
    In the event timeline, execution/supervision items carry event_type="Decision",
    indistinguishable from the merits ruling except via free-text description.
    Example: KlimaSeniorinnen v. Switzerland (Sabin.family.15540.0) has a 2025-03-06
    "Decision" event ("Committee of Ministers … supervision of execution") AFTER the
    2024-04-09 Grand Chamber merits judgment — so a naive "latest Decision" reads the
    wrong (later) date for the landmark.
    (Rare in our mirror — 1 case — but the modeling pattern is worth a distinct event
    category, e.g. "Execution" / "Supervision", so consumers don't conflate them.)


================================================================================
LOWER-CONFIDENCE / POSSIBLY-EDITORIAL-SCOPE
================================================================================

[5] OUTCOME NOT MACHINE-READABLE — no structured who-won / disposition field in our
    mirror (0% populated). May be intentional editorial scope; flagged because
    outcome is the first thing a litigator filters on.

[6] PARTIES NOT EXPOSED AS STRUCTURED FIELDS in the bulk/family record (plaintiff /
    defendant must be parsed from the title). The per-case detail pages may carry
    more structure — please validate.

[7] ONE IMPLAUSIBLE FILING DATE: Sabin.family.5916.0 ("Friends of Capital Crescent
    Trail v. Federal Transit Administration") filed 1970-01-26. Verify — possibly a
    genuinely old case, possibly a typo.


================================================================================
NOTED AS *NOT* ISSUES (to pre-empt false positives)
================================================================================
- 484 records have non-ASCII titles — these are overwhelmingly LEGITIMATE foreign
  case names (e.g. "Notre Affaire à Tous v. Total", "Brasilcom v. Ministério de
  Minas e Energia"). Not a defect. Only the duplicate mirrors among them (item 2)
  are an issue.
- All Sabin records in our mirror have non-empty summaries (good) and a valid
  jurisdiction code; no "XX" Sabin records.
- The "Analysis … Part 1/2/3 of 3" stub records we ingest are from the Climate
  Rights Database (University of Zurich) — a SEPARATE source, NOT Sabin/CPR.
