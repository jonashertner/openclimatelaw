================================================================================
LIKELY DATA ISSUES (worth validating)
================================================================================

[1] FILING DATES ARE YEAR-ONLY, STORED AS JANUARY 1  —  4,492 / 4,812 records (93%).
    filing_date is recorded at year granularity and defaulted to YYYY-01-01.
    Anyone who sorts or filters by filing date sees thousands of spurious
    "1 January" filings, and true within-year ordering is lost.
    Suggested check: does the platform expose date precision (a year-only flag),
    rather than a hard 01-01 date?

[2] APPARENT DUPLICATE / MIRROR RECORDS (same case, multiple family records).
    - Exact-title duplicates: "American Fuel & Petrochemical Manufacturers v.
      O'Keeffe" appears as BOTH Sabin.family.2153.0 AND Sabin.family.8546.0.
      (Verify whether these are true duplicates or distinct proceedings.)
    - Translation mirrors: the same case exists as separate English and
      translated records — e.g. "Juliana v. United States" (US) and "Juliana Youth
      v. United States of America (Jóvenes ex Juliana v. Estados Unidos de
      América)" (coded XA). They compete in search and split citation/influence.
    Suggested fix: a stable case-identity / dedup key (e.g. CPR import_id) to
    collapse duplicates and link language variants.

[3] INTERNATIONAL / REGIONAL CASES UNDER A CATCH-ALL JURISDICTION CODE "XA"  —  221 records.
    ICJ, ITLOS, ECtHR, IACtHR, etc. all share the non-granular jurisdiction_code
    "XA". (court_id distinguishes the body, but the jurisdiction code does not.)
    Both KlimaSeniorinnen v. Switzerland and the 2025 ICJ Advisory Opinion are "XA".
    Effect: you cannot filter international bodies by jurisdiction code alone.
    Suggested fix: body-specific codes (ICJ / ITLOS / ECTHR / IACTHR / …).

[4] POST-JUDGMENT / EXECUTION ITEMS TYPED AS "Decision" EVENTS.
    In the event timeline, execution/supervision items carry event_type="Decision",
    indistinguishable from the merits ruling except via free-text description.
    Example: KlimaSeniorinnen v. Switzerland (Sabin.family.15540.0) has a 2025-03-06
    "Decision" event ("Committee of Ministers … supervision of execution") AFTER the
    2024-04-09 Grand Chamber merits judgment — so a naive "latest Decision" reads the
    wrong (later) date for the landmark.
    (Rare in our mirror — 1 case — but the modeling pattern is worth a distinct event
    category, e.g. "Execution" / "Supervision", so consumers don't conflate them.)

ADDITIONAL SABIN (litigation) observations:

[8]  STATUS_CODE DISAGREES WITH metadata.status. Cases at a higher court read
     status_code="decided" while upstream metadata.status says "Pending"
     (Milieudefensie v. Shell, at the Hoge Raad) or "Dismissed" (Lliuya v. RWE).
     => one reconciled status vocabulary + a finality flag.

[9]  PROCEDURAL RULINGS TYPED AS "Decision" (broader than item [4]). Motions to
     remand, protective orders, discovery rulings — and bare "order" events with
     EMPTY descriptions — carry event_type="Decision", indistinguishable from a
     merits judgment. So decision_date can be a procedural docket date (e.g.
     Shoalwater Bay v. Exxon shows 2026-04-29 from a procedural order, merits
     unresolved). => type events merits / procedural / execution.

[10] DOCUMENT category_code OFTEN INACCURATE. Briefs, motions, notices — and a
     "Plaintiffs' Proposed Findings of Fact" (a party submission) — tagged
     "opinion"; many docs titled only "order" with no description. A consumer can
     mistake a party brief for the court's ruling. => distinguish court
     opinions/orders from party filings.

[11] claim_type TAXONOMY POLLUTED. Leaked metadata keys (year, keywords, blog,
     uncategorized, deciding-body, rights-at-stake, state-concerned, country names)
     mixed with real claim types; plus overlaps at multiple granularities
     (public-trust-claims-us vs public-trust-global vs public-trust-doctrine).
     => a controlled, hierarchical claim-type vocabulary.

[12] principal_laws MISSES SUMMARY-STATED BASES + LEAKS JURISDICTIONS. Asmania v.
     Holcim names Art. 28 ZGB / Art. 41 OR in its summary but has no principal_laws;
     "principal_law/United States" appears beside "jurisdiction/United States".
     => tighten concept extraction; keep jurisdictions out of principal-law.

NOTE on items [5]-[6] above (outcome / parties): we have since DERIVED both as a
demonstration — outcomes via a confidence-gated, quote-verified LLM classifier
anchored to the latest disposition; parties by caption parsing — labeled as OURS
(provenance), never as yours. A structured upstream field would still be valuable.


CLIMATE POLICY RADAR — Climate Change Laws of the World (CCLW) observations
(from ingesting the open ClimatePolicyRadar/all-document-text-data "Laws and
Policies" corpus, 5,347 laws / ~200 jurisdictions, and bridging it to litigation):

[C1] "LAWS AND POLICIES" IS PREDOMINANTLY NON-STATUTORY. Statutes/regulations are
     mixed with policies, plans, strategies, NDCs; the instrument type isn't exposed
     so a consumer can't filter "actual statutes". => an instrument-type field
     (statute / regulation / policy / strategy).

[C2] GENERAL STATUTES THAT CLIMATE LITIGATION TURNS ON ARE LARGELY ABSENT. The US
     Clean Air Act, NEPA, ESA, APA are not in CCLW (which focuses on climate-specific
     framework laws), so a case<->law bridge can't connect US cases to the laws they
     invoke. => note the scope, or extend coverage.

[C3] MULTILINGUAL STATUTES INTERLEAVE ALL LANGUAGES IN ONE DOCUMENT. e.g. the South
     Africa Climate Change Act 2024 interleaves all 11 official languages; reading by
     offset lands in the wrong language. => per-language segmentation / a language
     field per segment.

[C4] THE CASE<->LAW CONCEPT MAPPING ISN'T PUBLISHED. CPR's internal annotations
     linking cases to the laws they cite would make the litigation<->legislation
     bridge comprehensive (we match by exact title + jurisdiction today — high
     precision, partial: ~145 links). => share the concept mapping + import_ids.

[C5] NON-ISO GEOGRAPHY CODES (XKX Kosovo, EUR EU-wide, XAA) need special handling.
     => document the code set.
